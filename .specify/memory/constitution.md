### Quality & Design Principles
1. **Code Compatibility**: Ensure backward compatibility with older Guava methods where feasible to minimize immediate refactoring efforts.
2. **Testing Coverage**: Maintain a high level of automated test coverage to detect any issues arising from the upgrade.
3. **Stability Prioritization**: Prioritize system stability and performance throughout all phases of the upgrade process.
4. **Documentation**: Update all relevant documentation to reflect changes due to the library upgrade.
